This work proposes a novel demodulation method with a reference signal for processing measured responses of a damaged structure under random excitation by a CSLDV system. A baseline-free damage detection method is used to calculate curvatures of estimated undamped mode shapes and compare obtained curvatures with curvatures of polynomial-fitted undamped mode shapes.
