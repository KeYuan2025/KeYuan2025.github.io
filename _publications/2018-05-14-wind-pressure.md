The effects of façade appurtenances of high-rise building on local peak wind pressures were investigated through a series of wind tunnel experiments. Totally 21 appurtenances configurations simulated by thin horizontal splitter plates were studied. The area of large positive peak pressures of the models with appurtenances can be greatly smaller than the model without appurtenances.
