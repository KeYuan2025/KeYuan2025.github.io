This work proposes a novel mirror-assisted testing method for 3D CSLDV measurement that aims to measure 3D vibration of difficult-to-access areas of a structure without moving the 3D CSLDV system during the test, and stitch vibration of its different parts together to obtain its panoramic 3D ODSs.
