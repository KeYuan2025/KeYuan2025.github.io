Two novel methods that consider welded joints in the PTSP as equivalent stiffnesses of beam and shell elements are developed to create its beam-shell element models, which can significantly simplify its solid element model. In these methods, equivalent stiffnesses are calculated by matching rotational displacements of a welded joint created by beam-shell element models with that created by the solid element model.
