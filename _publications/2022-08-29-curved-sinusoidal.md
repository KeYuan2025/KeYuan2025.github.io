This study proposes a novel general-purpose 3D continuously scanning laser Doppler vibrometer (CSLDV) system to measure 3D full-field vibration of a structure with a curved surface in a non-contact and fast way. The proposed 3D CSLDV system consists of three CSLDVs, a profile scanner, and an external controller, and is experimentally validated by measuring 3D full-field vibration of a turbine blade with a curved surface under sinusoidal excitation and identifying its operating deflection shapes (ODSs).
