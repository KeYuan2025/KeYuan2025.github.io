This patent presents a three-dimensional continuously scanning laser Doppler vibrometry (3D CSLDV) method for zero-contact, rapid, and dense full-field measurement of three-dimensional and in-plane structural vibrations. The method enables measurement of full-field in-plane operating deflection shapes at high frequencies and low response amplitudes using three synchronously scanning laser heads and a calibrated measurement coordinate system.

The patented approach supports applications including experimental modal analysis, finite element model updating, and structural damage detection.
